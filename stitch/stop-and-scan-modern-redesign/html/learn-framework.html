Hi! I'm Amito. Let's learn how to spot tricky content together!

Step 1

STOP

When you encounter content that triggers a strong emotional reaction—whether it's outrage, excitement, or fear—the first thing to do is pause. Don't share, don't comment, just stop.

Step 2

SOURCE

Investigate the source. Who created this? Are they an expert or a random account? Look past the name to find their reputation and track record.

Step 3

CONTENT

Look for evidence within the content itself. Check for manipulation, AI artifacts, or missing context. Does the evidence actually support the claim being made?

Step 4

ALIGN

Find other sources. Does this information align with what trusted organizations or diverse experts are reporting? If it's only on one site, be careful.

Step 5

NOW REFLECT

Final check: Why was this content created? Was it to inform you, or to manipulate your opinion? Reflect on your own biases before you decide what to do next.
