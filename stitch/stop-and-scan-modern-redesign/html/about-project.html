About the Project

Stop & Scan is more than an educational tool; it's a sensemaking framework designed for trust calibration. We empower users to slow down, engage their reasoning, and navigate the complex landscape of digital information with confidence.

Let's look behind the curtain together!

Why a framework, not just a detector?

In an era of rapidly evolving synthetic media, only using a detection tool AI is a losing game. Technologies change, but human psychology and manipulation patterns remain consistent.

Stop & Scan shifts the focus from "Is this real?" to "How is this trying to influence me?". By teaching reasoning-based strategies, we provide a durable defense that outlasts any single algorithm and in turn improves the tool usage.
