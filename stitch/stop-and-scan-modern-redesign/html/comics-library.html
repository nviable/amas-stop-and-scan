Why Comics?

Visual narratives allow us to observe digital behaviors from a distance. By watching characters like Charleen react to sensationalist media, we can recognize our own impulses to "click and react." These comics are designed as literacy anchors—helping you translate complex sensemaking frameworks into everyday scrolling habits.
